Cookies and browser storage
We use only what the site needs to work, so no consent banner is required; we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
• Session and basket: to keep your basket and sign-in working.
• Security: to protect forms and stop spam.
• Payments: Stripe sets cookies to process payments securely and prevent fraud.
• Your lamp choices and forms: your lamp choices are saved in your own browser so they're still there if you come back. Anything you type on the Reserve and enquiry forms is forgotten when you close the tab or once you've sent it. You can clear your choices at any time with "Start again" or your browser settings.
Your rights
You can ask us at any time, free of charge, to:
• see the personal data we hold about you;
• correct anything that's wrong;
• delete your data, unless we must keep it (for example, order records for tax);
• restrict or object to how we use it;
• give you your data in a format you can take elsewhere.
Email info@lluniostudios.co.uk and we'll reply within one month.
If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.
Security and changes
Your data is sent over encrypted connections, uploaded files are kept private and accessible only to us, and card payments are handled entirely by Stripe. If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top.
