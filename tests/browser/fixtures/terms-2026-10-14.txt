5. Made to order: cancellations and refunds
You can cancel for a full refund until production starts. Once it has started, the order can't be cancelled or refunded, except for faulty goods (section 9).
• When production starts: we email you "Your lamp is being cast" on the day we begin. Until that email, your order is still open to cancellation.
• Cancelling before production: email info@lluniostudios.co.uk with your order number. We'll refund everything you've paid, including any deposit and delivery, within 14 days, to the card you paid with. If you paid a deposit, your casting slot is released.
• Why no cancellation after that: every lamp is cast to your chosen size and specification. UK law treats goods made to a customer's specification differently: the usual 14-day right to cancel online purchases doesn't apply to them (Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(b)).
• Your other rights are unaffected: if your lamp arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, section 9 applies however it was made.

9. Faulty goods: your legal rights
Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, your lamp must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If it isn't:
1. Within 30 days of delivery: you can reject it for a full refund.
2. Within 6 months: you can ask for a repair or replacement. Any fault is assumed to have been there on delivery unless we can show otherwise.
3. If a repair or replacement fails or isn't possible: you can have a refund or a price reduction. After 6 months, a deduction may be made for the use you've had.
4. Time limit: you can claim for up to 6 years from delivery in England and Wales, and 5 years in Scotland.
To make a claim, email info@lluniostudios.co.uk with your order number, a description and photos. We'll arrange and pay for collection of faulty goods.
10. Electrical safety, installation and use
Our lamps are built to Class II (double insulated) and should be used as described below.
• Indoor use only, in dry areas, unless we've agreed otherwise in writing.
• Hardwired installations, such as wall or ceiling fittings, must be carried out by a qualified electrician.
• Bulbs: E27 dimmable bulbs only. The built-in smart dimmer takes dimmable LED bulbs up to 150 W in total, or incandescent and halogen bulbs up to 200 W in total, per lamp.
• Don't modify the wiring, fittings or lamp. Faults caused by modification, incorrect installation or misuse aren't covered by section 9, but your rights for faults that were our responsibility are unaffected.
