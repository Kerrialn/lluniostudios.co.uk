# Merge plan: "Llunio Site v4 Unified" design into the live site

The design made in Claude Design ("Llunio Site v4 Unified") is the visual and UX spec for what this Symfony site becomes: one Lumen Forma configurator, a Reserve (request) route for larger or custom lamps, Commissions, Workshop, gift orders and a 6-email customer journey. The aim is to reskin and reshape the existing site to match the design, reusing what already works (orders, Stripe payments, shipping quotes, admin) rather than rebuilding it. The prototype is a spec only; its code is not copied in.

This file is the single source for the merge: decisions, step statuses, open questions and how to continue. Read it with `CLAUDE.md` (project conventions, pending content, notes for Kerrial).

### How the work is done (standing rules)

- Small steps: build one step (or sub-step), stop, say what to check, and **commit only when asked**.
- **Which checks to run (decided 13 October 2026, replacing "the full browser run before every commit"):** for **small changes** (wording, styling, single fixes), run only the checks related to the change (with the unit tests, PHPStan and code style when code changes). Run the **full browser run with `--clean`** only after **larger changes** (new features, anything touching payments, or changes across many pages), or **at the end of a day's work**.
- **Before committing, check `git status` and stage only this session's files** (decided 13 October 2026, after an unexpected Rector run was swept into `9237a7d`); ask about any changes that aren't ours.
- **Never move or discard uncommitted work without asking first**: no `git stash`, `git reset`, `git checkout` / `git restore` of files, `git clean`, or any other command that sets aside, overwrites or deletes uncommitted changes. Ask before each use, even to compare against the last commit (use `git diff` or `git show` instead). Decided 7 October 2026.
- **Match the prototype exactly**, comparing against it (screenshots and measured positions), not working from memory. Report any intended differences.
- **Real product photography only**: never generated, mocked up or substituted, and never another size's photos. Anything without a real photo shows "Image Coming Soon" (`templates/partials/_image_coming_soon.html.twig`). No filler or stretched edges across photos. Resizing and stripping metadata (including GPS) for the web is fine; the photo list and how to replace photos are in `CLAUDE.md` (Pending content).
- **British English** everywhere, including flash messages and emails (Basket, colour, aluminium, enquiry, Postcode, "7 October 2026", £).
- **"Dorset Studio"**, never "Bournemouth studio" (the design's text says Bournemouth). The studio's location, where a town is named, is "Bournemouth, Dorset" (decided 14 October 2026).
- **No postal address is ever published** (decided 14 October 2026): the workshop's address must never appear on the site, in an email, in the structured data, or in this repository (it is public); valuables are stored there.
- **Lead time: 1–3 weeks** for direct orders (decided 16 October 2026, was 7–14 days): `LeadTime` (`lead_time.label` in Twig), everywhere on the site and in every email, the Terms & Conditions (section 6: "Direct orders are dispatched within 1–3 weeks, depending on the casting schedule; quoted orders follow the estimated casting date in your quote."), the configurator's "Direct Order | 1–3 Week Dispatch", and the expected dispatch date suggested in admin (3 weeks from payment).
- **Gift dates (decided 16 October 2026):** any date from tomorrow can be chosen (basket and Reserve Your Design). A date sooner than the usual lead time plus delivery (3 weeks, then 2 working days: `GiftDate::earliest()`) sends the order to Reserve Your Design instead of checkout, as Custom choices and addresses outside the UK mainland do: the basket's button becomes "Reserve Your Design" with the note under it, "This date is sooner than our usual 1–3 week lead time. Reserve your design and we'll let you know if we can make it." (`BasketRules::GIFT_DATE_NOTE`), and the gift (date and where to send it) is carried into the request. A date before tomorrow: "Please choose a date from tomorrow onwards."
- **No size abbreviations** (XS, S, M, L, XL) anywhere: sizes are Studio, Gallery, Atelier, Estate, Monument.
- Migrations are **hand-written** (only the step's own changes; `doctrine:migrations:diff` picks up unrelated drift) and tested up, down and up again.
- Anything touching payments or the database gets a note on how to undo it.
- **Never ask to re-approve a decision already made** (decided 15 October 2026): anything that only enforces a recorded rule or an earlier answer is applied straight away and recorded here; only genuinely new choices (new sentences, changed meaning, layout or content) are brought for approval.
- **The voice (decided 15 October 2026, for the wording pass 11.5 and everything written after it):**
  - **"We"** for the studio and its processes ("We dispatch within 7–14 days", "We'll email you when it's on its way").
  - **"I"** when it's personally from Theo: conversations, enquiries, quotes, and anything signed by him ("I'll be in touch to talk through your space").
  - Warm, calm and confident; short sentences; plain words. No corporate or salesy language, no exclamation marks, no clichés.
  - Keep the handmade, made-to-order character: hand cast, made to order, Dorset Studio, each one unique.
  - British English. Always "Llunio Studios" in full. Always "lamp" (or "lamps") after a size name ("your Gallery lamp").
  - Greet customers by first name only ("Dear Ada,").
  - Every customer email that's personal or part of an order's journey is signed "All the best," / blank line / "Theo" / "@LlunioStuidos" (`EmailService::ENQUIRY_SIGNATURE`).
  - **"lamp" and "lamps" (decided 16 October 2026):** "lamp" for one lamp and "lamps" only when more than one was ordered, across every email and page, with the rest of each sentence matching (it/they, is/are, its/their). The order emails take their words from `Order::getLampWords()`.
  - **The way of getting in touch (decided 16 October 2026):** wherever wording says how the customer will be contacted, it matches the method they chose (email, phone or video call); For Your Project enquiries are answered by email.
  - **The signature's spelling, "@LlunioStuidos", is settled and correct.** Never flag or question it, in any file, check or message.
- **Every reply ends with a short handover summary** (decided 10 October 2026), so the session can be cleared at any point and a fresh one can carry on: what was done (with commit numbers), what's uncommitted, anything waiting for your decision or checks, and the exact next step. **This plan is kept up to date after every change.**
